# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Disagreement about designs is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and posting private data are not. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock discussions, warn participants or restrict participation. Enforcement should reflect severity, context and repeated behavior. Do not retaliate against someone who reports a concern.

The project maintainer is [wiseqingyang](https://github.com/wiseqingyang). For abuse on GitHub, use GitHub's private “Report content” channel. A project-specific private conduct contact must be confirmed before public launch and is tracked in the delivery plan. Do not publish someone else's personal information in an issue to make a report.

For technical vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
